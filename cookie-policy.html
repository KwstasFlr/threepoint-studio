1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They can help websites remember information and provide certain functionality.

With your choices respected through Google's consent system, we also use Google Analytics 4 to understand website usage and Google AdSense to support advertising on the website.

Necessary cookies may be used where required for basic website functionality, security or technical operation.

Google Analytics 4 may collect information about how visitors use the website, including pages viewed, interactions, approximate location derived from network information, device and browser data. Our Google Analytics measurement ID is G-01ET0K1H05.
