THE PLAYER GUIDE

ONE GUESS. A BETTER CLUE.

Game Multi Plex brings football, One Piece and gaming knowledge into short browser challenges. Start with a name, compare the feedback and use what you learn to narrow down the next guess. No download is needed; you can play as a guest.

01 · FOOTBALL02 · ONE PIECE03 · GAMING

01 · FOOTBALL

Find the hidden player in up to 10 guesses. Type a name and choose a player from the suggestions. The table compares nationality, highest recorded career market value, active or retired status, age and position with the target.

A check means a match; a cross means a difference. An up arrow means the target age or value is higher than your guess; a down arrow means it is lower. A question mark means a market value is unavailable, not zero.

For example, if your guessed active player is 28 and the age cell shows ↑, try an older active player who also matches the confirmed position. Retired players show “Retired” instead of their current age. Market value is an estimate, not a transfer fee.

Daily uses the day’s target and resets at midnight in Athens. Unlimited lets you start another round when you want. EUR and USD are display options for the same underlying value; the game shows the conversion date and source.

02 · ONE PIECE

Choose Daily or Unlimited to find a character through trait comparisons, with 10 standard guesses. Before you begin, choose anime or manga and enter the last episode or chapter you have reached. This limits the eligible character pool.

Use confirmed traits together. One matching characteristic rarely identifies the answer by itself. If the next guess breaks a confirmed match, it may spend an attempt without reducing the remaining possibilities.

“Who said it?” offers 20 short lines from 20 different characters, with 5 guesses per round. Identify the speaker, then check the episode or chapter reference after the answer is revealed. English and Greek wording is adapted for the game and may differ from a subtitle or published translation.

The progress setting reduces exposure to later characters and scenes; it cannot guarantee that every visible detail is spoiler-free. Keep your limit at your actual progress and avoid opening answer references until you are ready.

03 · GAMING

Classic contains 20 characters and gives you 6 guesses. Compare gender, franchise, game genre, debut year and signature weapon or fighting style. The year is the character’s video-game debut, not the release year of the latest sequel.

Suppose the genre matches but the franchise does not: keep the genre as a clue and try a character from another series. Combine it with the debut year and fighting-style feedback before making the next selection.

Quotes gives you 5 guesses to identify the speaker of one of 20 lines. Read the voice and context of the line rather than relying only on familiar words. The reveal names the character, the game and a reference so you can check the attribution.

Classic and Quotes keep separate rounds. Gaming is a practice challenge and currently does not award leaderboard points. You can move between the two modes without treating a quote guess as a Classic attempt.

BEFORE YOUR NEXT ROUND

How do Football points work?

A correct answer earns 5 points on the first guess, 4 on the second, 3 on the third, 2 on the fourth and 1 from the fifth onward. A Daily win adds 2 bonus points. A lost round earns no answer points.

The current online rankings cover Football. Logging in connects eligible results to your account; guest progress is kept locally on this browser.

What does round No2 mean?

The round number counts challenges in the current game and mode, not guesses. Each mode keeps its own number. Local progress can disappear if you clear browser storage or use another browser or device.

Can I play after using all my guesses?

Football and One Piece character modes may offer one extra guess through a rewarded ad when that option is available. If it is unavailable, use the reveal control to finish the round. Quote modes and Gaming use their stated attempt limits.

Where can I report an incorrect clue?

Send the game, mode, character or player name and the incorrect field through the studio contact page. Include a reference if you have one. That makes it possible to check the data rather than guessing what happened.
